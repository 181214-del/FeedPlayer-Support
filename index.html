隐私政策

FeedPlayer 是本地及个人文件服务器媒体播放器。媒体读取和播放主要在你的设备上进行；广告、系统购买、你选择的文件服务，以及你主动发送的支持邮件，涉及各自的数据处理。以下说明分别列明用途、接收方和管理方式。

1. 适用范围与开发者联系

本政策适用于 FeedPlayer iOS/iPadOS 应用及其支持页面。FeedPlayer 开发者的隐私与支持联系邮箱为 xie181214@gmail.com。应用没有 FeedPlayer 用户账号或开发者运营的云媒体库，也没有用于上传或分析你的媒体的开发者服务器。

2. 设备上的媒体、配置和记录

当你添加、浏览或播放媒体时，应用会读取你选择的文件、文件名、路径、大小、时长、缩略图、字幕和其他播放所需元数据。导入引用、收藏、服务器配置、播放位置、缓存、界面偏好和广告展示计时保存在本机，用于恢复访问、播放与设置。应用不会把你选择的媒体或服务器密码作为广告请求参数发送给 Google。

SMB/WebDAV 的地址、端口、账号、域及目录引用保存在本机。选择保存的密码使用 iOS Keychain，设置为仅此设备使用；未选择保存的密码仅用于当前会话。部分应用文档和偏好可能随你的系统备份保存；系统备份由 Apple 及你的系统设置管理。

播放和连接故障可能在设备系统日志中产生诊断记录。SMB 连接诊断还会保存在应用沙盒内，最多保留 3 个各不超过 1 MiB 的文件，按容量自动轮转清理；该目录排除系统备份，并通过字段白名单避免记录密码、原始地址和文件路径。应用没有自动上传这些日志到开发者服务器的功能。只有你主动提供日志或反馈时，开发者才会收到相应内容。

3. 相册、文件提供者和网络服务器

相册权限用于读取你选择的照片、视频和元数据。你可以允许有限照片访问，并在系统设置中调整范围；应用不修改或删除相册原件。若原件位于 iCloud，系统相册服务可能从 Apple 下载内容。通过“文件”选择的 iCloud Drive 或其他提供者也可能下载文件。

局域网权限用于发现 SMB 服务及连接局域网中的 SMB/WebDAV 服务器。连接你配置的服务器时，该服务器会收到网络连接信息（例如 IP 地址）、认证所需信息和请求的文件路径；它可能保存自己的访问日志。文件服务的运营者和存储位置由你选择，适用其自身政策。媒体不经过 FeedPlayer 开发者服务器。

WebDAV 支持 HTTPS 和 HTTP。HTTPS 使用系统证书验证；HTTP 不加密传输，请仅在可信网络使用。SMB 的传输保护取决于服务器与协商设置，请勿假设所有远程连接都加密。

4. 广告与隐私选择

如果你尚未购买永久去广告，应用可能使用 Google AdMob 展示广告，并使用 Google User Messaging Platform（UMP）提供广告隐私选择。Google AdMob 和 UMP 是独立第三方服务。

设备或应用信息、IP 地址、广告互动、诊断信息以及广告隐私选择，可能由 Google 及其广告合作伙伴根据其自身隐私政策处理。FeedPlayer 开发者不会通过自有服务器主动收集或存储这些广告数据。

在适用地区，你可以通过应用显示的同意界面或“广告隐私选项”查看和更改选择。购买永久去广告后，FeedPlayer 将停止发起应用内广告请求。

5. 购买、支持邮件和公开网站

永久去广告是通过 Apple StoreKit/App Store 完成的一次性购买。应用读取商品资料和经 Apple 验证的购买权益，用于去广告、恢复购买及反映撤销或退款状态。开发者不会收到你的完整银行卡资料。见 Apple 隐私政策。

如果你主动发送邮件，开发者和邮件服务提供者会处理你的邮箱、邮件正文及附件，用于回复、排查和处理隐私请求；请勿提供服务器密码、敏感媒体或与问题无关的信息。这些邮件只保留到处理请求及必要后续记录所需的时间，法律另有要求的除外；你可以通过上述邮箱请求访问、更正或删除。应用不自动把日志附加到邮件中。

应用内政策文本随安装包提供，阅读本身不需要联网。点击外部链接会打开对应服务；访问本支持网站时，托管方 GitHub Pages 可能处理 IP 地址和访问日志。网站没有我们添加的广告、分析脚本或追踪 Cookie。见 GitHub 隐私声明。

6. 保存、删除与撤回

  • 缓存：通过“设置 → 清空缓存”删除已缓存媒体与缩略图；再次浏览或播放可能重新产生缓存。缓存也会按容量策略清理。
  • 播放位置：关闭“设置 → 记录上一次播放位置”会停用并清除已保存的播放位置。
  • 导入项目、收藏与服务器：在对应列表移除。删除服务器会同时删除该配置关联的已保存密码。移除应用中的引用或缓存不会删除相册或远端服务器上的原始媒体。
  • 权限：在系统设置调整相册、局域网和跟踪权限；拒绝某权限只影响依赖该权限的功能。
  • 卸载：系统删除应用会移除其沙盒数据，但系统备份和 Keychain 项目不应被视为必然一起删除。需要删除已保存密码时，请先在应用中删除对应服务器。

本机记录持续保存到你移除、关闭相应记录或由缓存策略清理。Apple、Google、GitHub 和你选择的文件服务分别管理自己持有的数据，应用中的清理操作不能删除这些第三方记录。你可以通过第三方的隐私工具或联系渠道行使适用的权利。

7. 你的权利与未成年人

根据你所在地法律，你可能享有知情、访问、更正、删除、撤回同意、限制或反对处理等权利，以及向当地监管机构投诉的权利。关于开发者持有的反馈资料或本政策的问题，请联系上述邮箱；为保护你的资料，我们可能需要核实请求来源，且只索取完成核实所需的信息。

FeedPlayer 不专门面向儿童，也不以收集儿童个人信息为目的。未成年人应在监护人指导下使用媒体、网络服务和购买功能。如你认为向开发者提交的反馈中含有不应保留的儿童信息，请联系我们处理。

8. 政策更新

数据处理方式发生实质变化时，我们会更新本政策及日期，并在法律或系统规则要求时重新征求相应同意。应用内文本对应安装版本；本页面提供公开版本。可通过“设置 → 关于”查阅隐私政策、使用条款、第三方许可和支持方式。

Privacy Policy

FeedPlayer plays media from your device and file servers you select. Media processing primarily takes place on your device. Advertising, system purchases, file services and support emails involve the separate processing described below.

1. Scope and contact

This policy covers the FeedPlayer iOS/iPadOS app and its support pages. Contact the developer about privacy or support at xie181214@gmail.com. There are no FeedPlayer user accounts, developer-operated cloud media libraries, or developer servers for uploading or analyzing your media.

2. On-device information

When you add, browse or play media, the app reads your selected files, names, paths, sizes, duration, thumbnails, subtitles and playback metadata. Import references, favorites, server profiles, playback positions, caches, preferences and ad timing are stored locally to support these functions. The app does not include your selected media or server passwords as parameters in Google advertising requests.

SMB/WebDAV addresses, ports, accounts, domains and folder references are stored locally. Saved passwords use iOS Keychain with a device-only accessibility setting; unsaved passwords are used for the current session. Some app documents and preferences may be included in system backups according to your Apple settings.

Playback and connection diagnostics may appear in device system logs. SMB connection diagnostics are also stored in the app sandbox in up to three files, each limited to 1 MiB, and are automatically rotated by size. This directory is excluded from system backups, and a field allowlist prevents recording passwords, original addresses and file paths. The app does not automatically upload these logs to a developer server. The developer receives them only if you choose to share them.

3. Photos and services you select

Photos access lets the app read selected photos, videos and metadata. You can grant limited access and adjust it in system settings. The app does not modify or delete photo-library originals. Apple may download iCloud originals through Photos; iCloud Drive or other file providers may also download files you select.

Local-network access supports SMB discovery and SMB/WebDAV connections. Your server receives connection information such as your IP address, authentication information and requested paths, and may retain access logs. You choose the service and storage location; its own policy applies. Your media is not routed through developer servers.

WebDAV supports HTTPS with system certificate validation and unencrypted HTTP. Use HTTP only on trusted networks. SMB protection depends on server and negotiated settings; do not assume all remote connections are encrypted.

4. Advertising and privacy choices

If you have not purchased permanent ad removal, the app may use Google AdMob to show ads and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to provide advertising privacy choices. Google AdMob and UMP are independent third-party services.

Device or app information, IP address, ad interactions, diagnostic information, and advertising privacy choices may be processed by Google and its advertising partners under their own privacy policies. The FeedPlayer developer does not actively collect or store this advertising data on developer-operated servers.

Where applicable, you can review or change your choices through the consent screen or Advertising Privacy Options shown by the app. After permanent ad removal is purchased, FeedPlayer stops making in-app advertising requests.

5. Purchases, email and website

Permanent ad removal is a one-time Apple StoreKit/App Store purchase. The app reads product information and verified entitlements to enable ad removal, restore purchases and reflect revocations or refunds. The developer does not receive full payment-card information. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

If you email support, the developer and email provider process your address, message and attachments to reply, troubleshoot and handle privacy requests. Do not send server passwords, sensitive media or unrelated information. Emails are retained only for handling the request and necessary follow-up records, unless law requires otherwise. Request access, correction or deletion by email. The app does not automatically attach logs.

In-app policy pages are bundled and can be read offline. External links open their respective services. GitHub Pages may process IP addresses and access logs when you visit this website. We have added no ads, analytics scripts or tracking cookies to this site. See GitHub's Privacy Statement.

6. Retention, deletion and withdrawal

  • Use Settings → Clear Cache to remove cached media and thumbnails. Browsing or playback may recreate them; storage limits also trigger cache cleanup.
  • Turning off the setting that remembers playback positions disables and clears saved positions.
  • Remove imports, favorites and servers from their respective lists. Deleting a server also deletes its associated saved password. Removing app references or caches does not delete originals in Photos or remote services.
  • Manage Photos, local-network and tracking access in system settings. Declining permission affects the functions that require it.
  • Deleting the app removes its sandbox data, but does not guarantee removal of backups or Keychain items. Delete saved server profiles in the app first if you want their stored passwords removed.

Local records remain until you remove them, disable their recording, or the cache policy cleans them. Apple, Google, GitHub and your file services control their own records. Clearing app data does not delete those records; use each provider's privacy tools or contact channels.

7. Rights and minors

Depending on local law, you may have rights to information, access, correction, deletion, withdrawal of consent, restriction or objection, and to complain to a regulator. Contact the developer for requests about feedback data the developer holds. We may need to verify the request's origin using only information necessary for that purpose.

FeedPlayer is not directed specifically to children and is not designed to collect children's personal information. Minors should use media, network services and purchases with guardian guidance. Contact us if feedback sent to the developer contains children's information that should be removed.

8. Updates

We will update this policy and date when processing changes materially, and request new consent where law or platform rules require it.
