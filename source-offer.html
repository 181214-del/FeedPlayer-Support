Open-source libraries, sources and replacement · 2026-09-16

FeedPlayer uses AMSMB2 and MobileVLCKit for SMB access and media playback. These libraries and their use are covered by the LGPL; their dependencies retain their respective licenses. Complete copyright and license texts are available in the third-party notices, also bundled for offline reading.

FeedPlayer 使用 AMSMB2 和 MobileVLCKit 提供 SMB 访问和媒体播放。这些库及其使用受 LGPL 约束；库内其他组件分别保留自己的许可证。完整版权和许可可在第三方声明中离线阅读。

Modification and replacement / 修改与替换

To the extent the LGPL applies, you may modify the work for your own use and reverse engineer it to debug those modifications. FeedPlayer loads these libraries through separate AMSMB2.framework and MobileVLCKit.framework dynamic frameworks, which may be replaced with platform-, architecture- and interface-compatible modified versions.

See the tool instructions above for replacement, signing and installation steps. For a corresponding unencrypted application copy usable for library replacement, or for missing materials, contact xie181214@gmail.com.

在 LGPL 适用范围内，你可以为个人使用修改包含这些库的作品，并为调试修改进行逆向工程。FeedPlayer 通过独立的 AMSMB2.framework 和 MobileVLCKit.framework 动态加载这两个库；可用平台、架构及接口兼容的修改版本替换它们。

替换及签名安装步骤见上述工具说明。如需对应应用版本的未加密替换副本，或发现材料缺失，请联系 xie181214@gmail.com。
